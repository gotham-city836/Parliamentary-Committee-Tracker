Committee on Commerce

DRSC • Administered by Rajya Sabha

Structural Analysis

The committee scrutinizes Demands for Grants, Bills, and long-term policies of the Ministry of Commerce and Industry. Under Rules 268-277 of the RS, it possesses the mandate to summon industry bodies, review Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) frameworks, and evaluate international trade agreements.

JPC on Viksit Bharat Shiksha Adhishthan Bill

Ad-Hoc Joint Parliamentary Committee

Member

Structural Analysis & Composition

Formed in Dec 2025, this JPC is exclusively mandated to examine the bill intended to repeal the UGC, AICTE, and NCTE. It aims to create a unified apex higher education regulator, heavily impacting university autonomy.

Chairperson: Daggubati Purandeswari (BJP)

Total Strength: 30 Members (Joint Houses)

Dr. Trivedi was inducted in Feb 2026 due to his academic background (Ph.D. Engineering) to provide technical framing for STEM accreditation.

Status & Predictive Outlook

Current Phase: Transitioned into "Clause-by-Clause Consideration" as of late October 2026. Following extensive testimonies from Central and State University VCs in Q2/Q3, the committee is currently heavily deliberating Chapter IV (State Regulatory Exemptions).

Predictions for Next Draft:
  • Dilution of the clause allowing the center to unilaterally appoint State University oversight panels, favoring a concurrent approach.
  • Introduction of a separate sub-regulatory tier exclusively for deep-tech (AI/Quantum/Semiconductor) institutes to bypass standard accreditation lags.
  • Final report expected to be tabled in the Winter Session (Dec 2026).

Public Accounts Committee (PAC)

Financial • Joint Houses • Elected Members

Member

Structural Analysis & Composition

The apex audit committee. Governed by Rule 308 (LS), it scrutinizes government appropriation accounts and CAG reports. Crucially, RS members are elected annually via single transferable vote, not nominated.

Chairperson: K.C. Venugopal (INC - Opposition Chair Tradition)

Strength: 22 (15 LS, 7 RS)

Role: Dr. Trivedi acts as a primary ruling-party defense anchor during hostile audits.

Recent Audits & Predictions

Recent Scrutiny (126th Report - Aug 2026): Investigated the CAG Performance Audit on Border Roads Organization (BRO Phase III). The PAC rigorously questioned MoD officials over strategic tunneling delays in the Arunachal sector and sanctioned sweeping protocols for expedited ecological clearances.

Upcoming Docket (Predicted):
  • Reviewing the upcoming CAG audit on Highway Toll Collection Mechanisms (NHAI).
  • Examination of Telecom Spectrum Allocation procedures for initial 6G testbeds (FY25-26 compliance).

Historical Context & Legislative Impact (Term I)

A deep-dive into crucial deployments during the 2019-2024 tenure that forged Dr. Trivedi's current regulatory expertise and standing assignments.

JPC on Personal Data Protection Bill, 2019

Aug 2021 – Dec 2021

Served on the highly consequential Joint Parliamentary Committee that comprehensively overhauled India's initial digital privacy framework. Dr. Trivedi was instrumental during the contentious debates surrounding Section 35 (Exemptions for State Agencies), strongly advocating for national security carve-outs.

Legislative Legacy: His push for strict data localization requirements and hardware sovereignty within the committee laid the conceptual groundwork for the eventual, leaner Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act of 2023. This foundational experience directly catalyzed his elevation to the Commerce Committee Chairmanship in 2026, as data flow is increasingly treated as a critical trade commodity.

Committee on Energy

July 2020 – April 2024

Engaged in multi-year oversight of India's ambitious transition toward 500 GW of non-fossil fuel capacity. Dr. Trivedi actively participated in site visits to ultra-mega solar parks and nuclear facilities.

  • Flagship Contribution: Co-authored the critical report on the "Financial Health of State DISCOMs," pressing for the Revamped Distribution Sector Scheme (RDSS) and the enforcement of smart-metering targets to curb AT&C losses.
  • Policy Stance: Consistently advocated for grid stabilization technologies and hydrogen mission funding during Demands for Grants deliberations.

Committee on Papers Laid on the Table (Rajya Sabha)

Nov 2022 – April 2024

A specialized procedural committee ensuring executive accountability. Dr. Trivedi's tenure focused on reprimanding ministries for chronic delays in framing subordinate legislation (rules and regulations) after a parent Act was passed by Parliament. His scrutiny improved compliance rates for tabling annual reports of PSUs and central universities, sharpening his procedural mastery of the Upper House.
